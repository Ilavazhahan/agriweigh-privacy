AgriWeigh Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the AgriWeigh Android app (package name com.athiravan.agriweigh) handles your information. AgriWeigh is developed and operated by Athiravan Scale, Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India ("we", "us", "our").

Request deletion of your data (AgriWeigh)

AgriWeigh does not have its own user accounts and we do not keep your business data on our servers. You can delete your data yourself, or ask us to help.

Steps to delete your data:

  1. Data on your phone: delete weighings and traders inside the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → AgriWeigh → Storage → Clear data (or uninstall the app). This permanently removes all records stored on the device.
  2. Your Google Drive backup: open Google Drive, find the AgriWeigh backup file (agriweigh-backup.db), and delete it, then empty the Drive trash.
  3. Google sign-in access: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove AgriWeigh's access.
  4. Need help? Email athira.scale@gmail.com with the subject "AgriWeigh data deletion". We will reply within 7 days.

What is deleted: weighing records, trader details, scale settings, licence data on the device, and the backup file in your Google Drive.

What is kept: nothing. We hold no copy of your data, so there is no retention period on our side. Google may keep Drive data for a short period under its own policies.

1. Summary

  • AgriWeigh stores your weighing, trader and report data on your device.
  • We do not run servers that collect your business data, and we do not sell your data.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • If you choose to use backup, your data is saved to your own Google Drive using your Google account.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, SMS, or call logs.

3. Device permissions

  • Bluetooth (scan, connect and legacy Bluetooth): to find and connect to your weighing scale. Depending on your Android version, the system may group Bluetooth scanning with nearby-device access. The app does not use this to determine your location.
  • USB: to read weight data from a scale connected with a USB serial cable.
  • Internet and network state: only for Google sign-in and backup.
  • Foreground service, wake lock and run at startup: to run backup and background tasks reliably, including after the device restarts.

4. How we use information

Information is used only to provide the features of the app: recording weighings, managing traders, producing reports, connecting to your scale, activating your licence, and backing up and restoring your data. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • Google: if you sign in and use backup, the app uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API to save your backup file to your own Google Drive. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Reports you share: if you choose to share or export a report, it goes to the app or person you select. We do not control that destination.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, although we hold no copy of your business data.

6. Data storage, security and retention

Your records stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Your backup remains in your Google Drive until you delete it. Data sent to Google during sign-in and backup is encrypted in transit. You are responsible for keeping your device and Google account secure.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can use the app without signing in to Google if you do not want backup.
  • You can delete weighings and traders inside the app, or remove all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling.
  • You can delete your backup file from your Google Drive, and revoke the app's access in your Google Account permissions.
  • To ask about your data or request help with deletion, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

AgriWeigh is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Third-party libraries

The app uses standard Android and Google libraries (such as Google Play Services for sign-in, Room for local storage, WorkManager for background tasks) and an open-source USB serial library for scale communication. None of these are used by us for advertising or analytics.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
